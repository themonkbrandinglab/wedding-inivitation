# Dawn & Lotus Invitation

Create a premium, cinematic, photorealistic South Indian Tamil wedding e-invitation website for:

ARUNA × ARAVIND

A TALE OF DAWN & LOTUS

ஒரு புதிய தொடக்கம்

The entire experience must be inspired by Madurai Meenakshi Temple, traditional Tamil Nadu weddings, South Indian craftsmanship, and the meanings associated with the names Aruna and Aravind.

ABSOLUTE VISUAL REQUIREMENT

Everything must look physically realistic and premium.

Do NOT create a cartoon, flat illustration, generic AI-art wedding template, plastic-looking 3D objects, or overly stylized fantasy environment.

The visual target should be:

Photorealistic architectural visualization + luxury Indian wedding cinematography + physically accurate materials + subtle cinematic animation.

CENTRAL STORY

Use the meanings of the names as the visual foundation.

ARUNA

Represented by:

dawn • reddish-golden light • sunrise • warmth

ARAVIND

Represented by:

lotus • beauty • purity • blooming

The recurring visual story is:

Darkness → Deepam → Dawn → Lotus → Temple → Wedding

The entire invitation should feel like a journey from darkness into light.

REALISTIC DEEPAMS

The deepam is one of the most important elements.

Use handcrafted antique brass South Indian kuthuvilakku.

The brass must show:

realistic metal reflections

subtle scratches

aged areas

darker recessed carvings

polished highlights

fingerprints / tiny imperfections

realistic roughness

The flame must behave like a real oil flame.

Include:

cotton wick

oil

blue base

yellow core

orange outer flame

natural flickering

changing shape

realistic warm light

The flame must dynamically illuminate the environment.

It should create:

moving highlights

subtle shadows

brass reflections

warm light falloff

realistic reflections on nearby surfaces

Add extremely subtle smoke.

Never use a static flame image.

REALISTIC FLOWERS

All flowers must look like real fresh flowers, not digital stickers.

Use:

Jasmine

Individual white petals with natural variation and soft translucency.

Lotus

Realistic layered petals with subtle pink/white gradients.

Marigold

Detailed dense petals.

Roses

Natural petal folds and imperfections.

Other traditional flowers

Use selectively according to each ceremony.

Add realistic:  

flower shadows

petal translucency

small imperfections

slight movement

natural color variation

moisture / freshness

Create realistic jasmine garlands with individually connected flowers.

Do not make garlands look like repeating textures.

REALISTIC ENVIRONMENT

Every scene should have physical depth.

Use:

foreground

middle ground

background

Example:

Foreground: deepams + flowers

Middle: mandapam

Background: temple pillars + gopuram

Use cinematic depth of field.

Lighting should behave naturally.
